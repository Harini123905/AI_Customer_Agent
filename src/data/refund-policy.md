# Refund Policy – ShopEasy

## 1. Return Window
- Refunds can be requested within 30 days of the delivery date.
- Requests made after 30 days must be denied.

## 2. Non-Refundable Items
- Digital products, software licences and gift cards are never refundable.
- Items bought in a "Final Sale" or clearance offer are never refundable.
- Opened hygiene items (for example earphones, cosmetics, innerwear) are not refundable, unless they are defective.

## 3. Item Condition
- Items must be unused and in original packaging.
- Items that are damaged or used by the customer are not refundable.

## 4. Defective or Wrong Items
- If the item is defective or the wrong item was delivered, a refund can be requested within 60 days of delivery.
- The customer must clearly describe the problem.

## 5. Order Status
- Orders that have not yet been delivered cannot be refunded; they should be cancelled instead.
- Orders that were already refunded cannot be refunded again.

## 6. Refund Limits
- Refunds of up to ₹10,000 can be approved automatically by the agent.
- Refunds above ₹10,000 must be escalated to a human manager.
- A customer may receive at most 3 refunds in any 12-month period.

## 7. Refund Method
- Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.
